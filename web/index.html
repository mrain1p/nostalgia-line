Review

Placements the app is not confident about. These are held back from the export until you look — in the spec's own testing, 22 of 32 uncertain guesses turned out wrong. They group by cause, so this is usually a handful of decisions rather than a long list.

Station mapping

Point each real-world station at a NostalgiaTV channel. HBO feeds H.B.Yo Min, AMC feeds A.M.Sea, and so on. Mapping one station moves every title on it at once, which makes this the fastest way to clear uncertainty. Worst-covered first.

Channel artwork

Most channels already show the real network's logo, pulled from TMDB during the scan — nothing to set up. Upload images or a zip to override any of them, or mount a folder read-only at /logos. Filenames match by number, channel name, or the network being parodied.

Custom channels

Channels you created yourself in NostalgiaTV. Tell Nostalgia Line what each one is — a real station TMDB knows (G4, TechTV, Toonami), or an existing channel whose content it should borrow — and it will fill automatically.
